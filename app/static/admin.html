Dataset installation

This installs the 9 IR Parquet files from Zenodo record 16417648 (about 8.1 GB) and builds a local search index. Reserve at least 20 GB of free disk space. These are computed spectra, not experimental identification.

By starting the installation you confirm that you accept CDLA-Permissive-2.0 for the downloaded dataset.
